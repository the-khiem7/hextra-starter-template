When you generate a repository from this template, you already have a working place to publish notes, essays, and project write-ups.

## What this sample demonstrates

{{% steps %}}

### Start with a short summary

Open with one or two paragraphs that explain what the article is about and why it matters.

### Break the article into sections

Use headings to make scanning easier for readers who are searching for one specific point.

### Add examples when they help

Include commands, code snippets, screenshots, or short references only where they improve understanding.

{{% /steps %}}
